## Accessibility

Focus a chart with Tab or click it. Use Left and Right to inspect categories, Home and End to jump to the first or last, and Escape to dismiss inspection. Touch a category to inspect its values. Pointer, touch and keyboard use the same Hyozu tooltip data.

Keep series names descriptive and provide a visible textual summary alongside important metrics. Chart inspection does not replace a text or table alternative for readers who cannot use a visual plot.
