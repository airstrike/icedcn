## Composition

Build the options from `select::group`, `select::label` and `select::item`. The select manages its trigger, list and selected value.

```text
Select
├── SelectTrigger
│   └── SelectValue
└── SelectContent
    ├── SelectGroup
    │   ├── SelectLabel
    │   ├── SelectItem
    │   └── SelectItem
    ├── SelectSeparator
    └── SelectGroup
        ├── SelectLabel
        ├── SelectItem
        └── SelectItem
```
