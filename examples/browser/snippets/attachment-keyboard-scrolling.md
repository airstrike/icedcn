### Keyboard scrolling

Interactive cards expose separate keyboard-focusable buttons for their trigger and actions. The component browser uses Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls. Enter or Space activates a focused icedcn button.

Moving focus to an off-screen file action scrolls it into view. An overflowing group is itself a tab stop. Use Left and Right to scroll it, or Home and End to reach either edge. These keys also work for a strip of presentational attachments.
