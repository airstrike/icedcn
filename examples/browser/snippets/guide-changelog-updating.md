## Track an update

Use the [repository history](https://github.com/airstrike/icedcn/commits/HEAD) to inspect changes beyond the entries on this page. Compare the commits and lockfile between the revision your application uses and the revision you plan to adopt.

After updating, run your application's checks and exercise its stateful controls: focus, keyboard selection, disabled state, open overlays, and retained values across rebuilds. Recompile any copied recipes against the updated public API. The [Cargo guide](icedcn://guide/cargo#build-and-verify) lists this workspace's checks.
