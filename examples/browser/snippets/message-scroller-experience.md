## What Makes a Great Streaming Chat Experience

Anchor the first row of a new turn near the top so the reader can read the question and follow the answer as it grows. A small peek at the previous reply gives the turn context. In a group conversation, an event such as someone joining can start a turn.

Enable live-edge following for a streaming reply when that suits the conversation. Once the reader scrolls away, keep their place. Use an end button to make new output discoverable, and let them return when they choose. Stable row keys preserve context through prepends, text wrapping and retries.

A saved thread can open at its beginning, its latest output or its last anchored turn. For long conversations, give bookmarks, search results or an outline a direct jump to a message key. Load older messages in batches and keep focus on the control the reader is using. Application announcements can report a completed reply or error once, after a burst of updates.
