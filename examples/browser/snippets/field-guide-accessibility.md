## Accessibility

Give every control a visible label. Clicking a label focuses its text input or textarea, and toggles a checkbox, switch or radio. A select label places focus on its trigger. Press Enter or Space to open it, then use the arrow keys and Enter to choose an option. A slider’s keyboard arrows adjust the focused thumb.

Keep each field’s description and error close to the control. Group related choices under one legend, and give each choice a distinct label. On a choice card the whole padded surface is clickable.
